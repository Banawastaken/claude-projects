SSRN Anomaly Backtests

37 of the most-cited trading strategies circulated on SSRN, rebuilt from public data and run from their first available month to today. The pattern across all of them: strategies earn much less after their paper is published, and most of the classic stock-selection anomalies have lost money over the past ten years.

How this was built, and what it leaves out

Method

  • Stock-selection anomalies use the long-short portfolios in Kenneth French's library (CRSP data, from 1926 where available): top quintile minus bottom quintile, value-weighted, or the published factor itself. BAB, QMJ, time-series momentum and value & momentum everywhere come from AQR's data library.
  • Rotation, timing and calendar rules are built here from those returns or from adjusted Yahoo ETF prices. Signals use data available at the close of month (or day) t and trade in t+1.
  • Costs. Rules built here pay 5 bp per unit of ETF turnover and 1 bp per side for daily in/out trades. Library portfolios are gross of costs, as published. High-turnover anomalies such as short-term reversal and momentum would lose a large share of their premium to costs.
  • Equity curves and CAGR assume idle and collateral cash earns the T-bill rate. The publication split uses the journal year (or SSRN year for working papers).

Not covered

  • Literally every SSRN strategy. SSRN holds over a million papers and several hundred documented anomalies. This ledger covers the canonical ones that free public data can reproduce faithfully.
  • Firm-level signals (PEAD, analyst revisions, short interest, insider trades, text/NLP) need CRSP/Compustat, I/B/E/S or similar licensed stock-level data.
  • Options, futures and intraday strategies (variance risk premium, FOMC pre-announcement drift, intraday momentum) need options chains, futures rolls, or event calendars not available here.
  • Survivorship and look-ahead. French and AQR portfolios are survivorship-free. The ETF tests start when each ETF launched, which shortens them. One look-ahead bug (seasonality) was found and fixed during the build.
